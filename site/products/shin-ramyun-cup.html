About this ramyeon

The same spicy beef and mushroom broth as the original Shin pack, portioned into a single serve cup that needs nothing but a kettle. Thinner noodles than the bag version so they soften in three minutes flat, which makes this the default choice for hostel rooms, office desks and late shifts.

How to cook

Fill to the inside line with boiling water, close the lid and wait 3 minutes. No pot, no draining.
