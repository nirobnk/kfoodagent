About this ramyeon

Nongshim's official take on the Toowoomba pasta trend that ran through Korean social media. Springy noodles get coated in a thick, milky cheese sauce carrying the Shin chilli underneath, lifted with garlic, mushroom and parsley. Rich enough to feel like a meal on its own, and far and away the most requested of the newer packs.

How to cook

Leave about four spoons of water when draining. The starch in it is what makes the sauce cling properly.

Ingredients & allergens

Wheat flour, palm oil, potato starch, cream powder, cheddar cheese powder, parmesan cheese powder, milk powder, garlic, red chilli powder, black pepper, dried parsley, shiitake mushroom, sugar, salt.

Allergens: Contains wheat, soy and milk. May contain egg, fish and beef.
