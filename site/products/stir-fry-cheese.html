About this ramyeon

The same drained stir fry format as the spicy version, with a generous cheddar powder sachet added at the end. The cheese rounds off the chilli without cancelling it, so the heat arrives later and softer. A good bridge for anyone stepping up from Chapagetti towards the properly hot packs.

How to cook

Drain hard, stir the sauce through first, then sprinkle the cheese powder over the top so it melts in streaks rather than dissolving.

Ingredients & allergens

Wheat flour, palm oil, potato starch, cheese seasoning powder (cheddar cheese, whey powder, milk), spicy stir-fry sauce mix, chilli seasoning oil, garlic, dried carrot, dried fish cake, sugar, salt.

Allergens: Contains wheat, soy, milk and fish. May contain egg and shellfish.
