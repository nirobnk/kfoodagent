About this drink

The Korean convenience store classic, in the 200ml carton that comes with a straw taped to the front and carries the same fat pot-shaped bottle on its label — a design that has barely changed since 1974. Mellow and creamy with a gentle banana sweetness rather than a candy one, and enough real milk in it to actually be filling. The single most recognisable drink in Korea.

How to serve

Best served cold, straight from the fridge.

Ingredients & allergens

Milk 60%, water, sugar, skim milk powder 1.9%, whey powder (milk), banana juice concentrate 0.32%, artificial flavours, emulsifier (mono- and di-glycerides of fatty acids), stabilizers (carrageenan, guar gum), colours (tartrazine, sunset yellow FCF).

Allergens: Contains milk.
